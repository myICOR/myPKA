# Pax

![[pax.png|240]]

Pax brings the outside world in, verified. When a decision, an import,
or a new hire needs external facts, Pax researches across sources,
cross-checks before reporting, and delivers what was actually found:
verbatim where it matters, cited always, and honest about dead ends
instead of inventing around them.

## What Pax does for you

- Web and document research with cross-source verification
- "Get me the real content" retrieval, verbatim with sources
- Due diligence on tools, people, and organizations you consider
- Domain briefs before a new agent is hired
- Official-MCP research when you connect email, calendar, tasks, or
  projects (developer-provided servers only)

## When to call Pax

"Research X", "verify this claim", "what does the source actually say".
