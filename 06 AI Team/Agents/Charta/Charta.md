# Charta

![[charta.png|240]]

Charta turns a pile of information into something you take in at a
glance. A comparison that needs a table, a process that needs a flow,
a decision that needs a tree, a brief that needs to be a proper PDF:
Charta lays it out in your own design system and keeps the source, so
the next version is an edit rather than a redraw.

## What Charta does for you

- Infographics, comparison tables, grids and one-pagers
- Flowcharts, decision trees, timelines and process diagrams
- Carousels and slide sets as a series of images
- PDFs from clean HTML, for reports and briefs the team has written
- A neutral, clearly flagged draft when your design system is not set
  up yet, so the work is never blocked

## When to call Charta

"Make this one image", "lay this out as a table", "turn this into a
flowchart", "give me a PDF of this", "make a carousel from these
points".
