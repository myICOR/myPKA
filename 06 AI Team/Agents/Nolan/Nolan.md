# Nolan

![[nolan.png|240]]

Nolan hires. When you need a role nobody on the team covers, the answer
is never "no": Nolan drafts the new agent's contract and bio, sets its
boundaries against the existing agents, and adds it to the roster after
your approval. The team grows exactly as fast as your needs demand.

## What Nolan does for you

- Turns "I wish the team could do X" into a hired, contracted agent
- Drafts both agent files: the user-facing bio and the AGENT.md
  system prompt
- Keeps the [[agent-index]] roster accurate
- Designs boundaries so agents never fight over the same work

## When to call Nolan

"Can the team do X?", "hire me a specialist for Y", "import this
skill I found".
