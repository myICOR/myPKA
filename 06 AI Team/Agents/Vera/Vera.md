# Vera

![[vera.png|240]]

Vera is the last set of eyes before something leaves your hands. She
checks every visual and every screen against your design system and
against accessibility standards, and she tells you plainly what blocks
it, what should be fixed, and what can wait. Every problem she names
comes with the fix, and she checks again once it is done.

## What Vera does for you

- Checks an infographic, image, PDF or slide before you send it
- Reviews a new page or component before it ships, and reruns your
  project's own checks on a code change before it is merged
- Tests contrast, text size, keyboard use and colour-blind safety
- Checks print files for bleed, resolution and colour space
- Asks whether the person receiving it will understand it without
  context
- Tests her own checks first, and lists what she did not check
- Gives one clear verdict: APPROVED, CONDITIONAL or BLOCKED

## When to call Vera

"Check this before I send it", "is this ready to ship", "is this
accessible", "the slides look inconsistent". Larry also calls her
before any visual or UI work is handed to someone else.
