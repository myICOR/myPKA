# Felix

![[felix.png|240]]

Felix builds the part of your app people actually touch. Buttons,
forms, pages, dashboards: he reads your codebase first, matches how it
already works, and ships components that follow your design system,
work from the keyboard and load fast. Your code stays in your project
folder; Felix never puts it in your vault.

## What Felix does for you

- Builds a new component or page, with every state handled (loading,
  empty, error, success, disabled)
- Fixes UI that is broken, looks off, or behaves strangely
- Moves hardcoded colours and sizes onto your design tokens
- Makes a slow page fast and an inaccessible one usable from the
  keyboard and a screen reader
- Builds node-graph editors (flow builders, diagram canvases)
- Fixes a bug together with a test that proves the fix
- Writes the plan for a bigger frontend change before any code moves

## When to call Felix

"Build me a settings page", "this form is broken on mobile", "refactor
this onto our design system", "why is this page so slow", "tabbing
through this does not work".
