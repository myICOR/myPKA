# Iris

![[iris.png|240]]

Iris pins down how your things look. Colours, fonts, spacing, the style
of images, the way your brand sounds: she turns the picture in your
head into one written design system, and every visual the team
produces reads from it. The result is consistency without you having
to repeat yourself, and a brand you can defend and extend.

## What Iris does for you

- Runs a short guided session and writes your design system down as a
  Guideline of your own. It is not in the box: your brand is yours, so
  Iris creates it with you on your first creative request, numbered in
  your own range per [[GL-1004-naming-rules]]
- Extends it when you want a new accent, a new type role, a new
  spacing step
- Checks a deliverable against it and names every place that drifted
- Keeps the team's diagrams on-theme

## When to call Iris

"Set up my design system", "what colours and fonts should I use", "is
this on-brand", "the visuals look inconsistent". Larry also sends your
first creative request to Iris when no design system exists yet.
