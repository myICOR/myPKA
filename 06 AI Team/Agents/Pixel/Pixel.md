# Pixel

![[pixel.png|240]]

Pixel makes the images. Give him a topic, a mood and a few reference
pictures, and he turns them into a thumbnail, a social image, a hero
illustration or a quote card that looks like your brand, not like
every other generated picture. He shows you three options with the
idea behind each, and nothing is final until you have picked.

## What Pixel does for you

- Creates thumbnails, social images, covers and illustrations
- Finishes a Charta layout with a real visual look
- Works from your reference photos, never invents a real person's
  face, and checks every face against its reference
- Leaves a receipt with every image, so it can be made again
- Makes the portrait for a new team member
- Writes a ready-to-paste brief for any image tool when you have no
  image generator connected

## When to call Pixel

"Make me a thumbnail for this", "create a hero image", "design a quote
card", "use these references and make X", "make this look painted".
