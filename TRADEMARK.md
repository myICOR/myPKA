# Trademark Policy

Our licenses are generous with the words and the structure. They do not cover our
name. This page explains where the line sits, in plain terms.

license. These marks were never in its scope.

## What you may do, without asking

- **Say what your work is built on.** "Built on the myPKA™ Scaffold by
  Paperless Movement® / ICOR®." "A myPKA setup for architects." "My ICOR®-based
  workflow."
- **Use the names in ordinary descriptive speech**: articles, videos, courses,
  talks, comparisons, reviews, tutorials, and criticism.
- **Keep the attribution notices** that the license already requires.
- **Use myPKA commercially**, including in and for your business, and say so.

This is nominative fair use. It is how trademarks are supposed to work, and we
are not going to chase anyone for it.

## What needs our written permission

- **Naming your product after ours.** A tool, app, service, template, or course
  called "myPKA Pro", "ICOR Plus", "Paperless Movement for X", or anything a
  reasonable person could mistake for an official product.
- **Using our logos or wordmarks as your branding**: your logo, your app icon,
  your product name, your company name, your domain.
- **Implying endorsement, affiliation, partnership, or certification** that does
  not exist. No "official", "certified", "authorized", or "partner" unless it is
  true and we said so in writing.
- **Domain names and social handles** built on the marks.
- **Merchandise** carrying the marks.

## The line in one sentence

**Use the marks to say what your work is built on. Do not use them to say who
your work is by.**

## Modified versions

If you distribute a modified myPKA, ShareAlike already requires you to license it
under CC BY-SA 4.0 and to indicate that changes were made. On the trademark side,
please also make clear it is **your** version and not ours, so users are not
misled about who maintains it and who supports it.

Good: "Acme Vault, built on the myPKA™ Scaffold by ICOR®. Not affiliated with
Paperless Movement S.L."

Not good: "myPKA by Acme."

## Asking

Requests, edge cases, OEM licensing, and branded redistribution:
**contact@myicor.com**. If you are unsure, ask. A short email is cheaper than a
rebrand.

## Enforcement posture

We enforce the marks to stop confusion about origin, not to police conversation.
Descriptive and critical uses are welcome, including uses we do not like. What we
act on is someone passing our work off as their own product, or their work off as
ours.
